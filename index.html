Someone is dead. Their phone is still unlocked. Read the messages, question the suspects, tap the details that matter, and name the killer yourself.

A detective game for Android, played entirely inside a phone. Six cases now, a new Daily Cold File every day.

Get it on Google PlaySupport & FAQ

How you investigate

  • Chat with suspectsEvery character has their own voice. Your replies change what they let slip.
  • Present evidenceConfront a liar with the exact photo, log or message that breaks their story.
  • Search crime scenesIllustrated, zoomable photos with details only a careful eye will find.
  • Crack locked phonesWork out the passcode from clues hidden elsewhere.
  • Pull the red stringPin clues to a corkboard and connect them.
  • Name the killerFill in who, how, when and why. No multiple choice.

Fair by design

No ads. No energy bars or timers. Hints are free and unlimited. No account and no sign-in. Everything runs on your phone, offline. Extra cases are one-time purchases through Google Play, and the Season Pass is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The game text is in English.
